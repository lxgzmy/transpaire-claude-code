# workflows/ — Sales Estimation

**All proposed, none built.** These are the seven use cases from the discovery of
27 August 2026 ([../docs/01-discovery-summary.md](../docs/01-discovery-summary.md)),
awaiting the Sales Estimating Manager's confirmation of priorities. Each one gets
its own spec file here before anything is authored, and no skill is built until
the rules it depends on are transcribed into `../rules/` and reviewed (org rule:
propose before building).

| # | Proposed workflow | Depends on | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lot and DA research brief — zoning, overlays, DCPs, DA history (superseded documents flagged), EPA information; every claim cited to its source | Confirmed list of DA portals per region; Archistar access decision | Proposed |
| 2 | Pricing request intake and completeness check — land contract, 88B and 10.7 present, current, matching the lot; chase-up email drafted; job folder built and filed | Completeness rules transcribed; job-folder conventions for `ESTATES INFORMATION` | Proposed |
| 3 | Land contract / 88B / 10.7 review checklist — flagged summary a person verifies | Checklist transcribed from the manager's existing practice | Proposed |
| 4 | Variation writing and a variation register — drafting from a priced input; pricing assistance later | Variation rules and rate sources settled; sample variations collected | Proposed |
| 5 | Priced-job finder — by lot, address, client or estate, regardless of LGA folder | Nothing beyond read access | Proposed |
| 6 | Price response email drafts — from the completed costing, using existing templates | Template emails collected | Proposed |
| 7 | SCR validation checks — cross-check register entries against job costing sheets | Job costing sheet and SCR samples | Proposed (later) |

**Boundaries (from discovery, standing):** site costs stay manual, Bluebeam
pricing markups stay with the manager, nothing prices a job on its own, and every
output is a draft with evidence for human review.
